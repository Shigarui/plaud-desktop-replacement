# Master Speaker Index — BIGGBY COFFEE Field Operations Plaud Library

Compiled for Grayson Terry, Field Operations Manager, BIGGBY COFFEE.

## Scope and methodology

- Source: all REC recordings in the Plaud library, excluding the `Trim`, `Z-Pre Trimmed`, `Personal`, `2- To Do`, `Import`, and `Flagged, Speaker Audit` folders (overlong raw files, non-Biggby personal recordings, and recordings with only unresolved "Speaker N" placeholders).
- 142 distinct named individuals were found tagged in recording titles across the library.
- Per user instruction, this package includes only speakers who either (a) appear in **5 or more** qualifying recordings, or (b) appear in **at least one** recording from the last 3 weeks (since 2026-09-22), **and** who have a transcript diarization segment actually attributed to their name (not just named in the recording title with the underlying speech still labeled "Speaker N").
- **41 speakers** met this bar and are included below with full profile cards and audio clips.
- 8 additional individuals were named in a recording title (Josh, Chris, John Arthur, Jody, Brenda, Aaron, Alex, Kathy) but every transcript segment for that recording still used an unresolved "Speaker N" label — i.e., the title names a participant but diarization never confirmed which turns are theirs. These were excluded from audio/acoustic profiling to avoid misattributing a clip to the wrong person; they would need manual speaker-audit confirmation in Plaud first.
- Audio clips are single representative segments (continuous diarized turns, 43–90 seconds) pulled from one recording per speaker — not multi-recording composites.
- Acoustic fingerprints (pitch, voice quality, speech rate) are measured from that one clip using librosa (pYIN pitch tracking, spectral/energy features) cross-checked against transcript word timing — single-sample estimates, not multi-recording validated voiceprints. Speech rate is derived from transcript word counts and will run a bit high relative to true speaking rate since transcript timing compresses some pause time.
